## Notes for App Review

```text
Littlemark has no account or login, and no in-app purchases.

All moments (photos, videos, voice notes, text) are stored only on the device. The app does not use iCloud in this version.

Location (when in use) is used only while creating or editing a moment, to look up the place name (Apple Maps geocoding) and the weather (WeatherKit). The Apple Weather mark and legal attribution link are shown wherever weather appears.

Speech recognition runs on device (requiresOnDeviceRecognition) to transcribe voice notes.

To try it: tap +, choose Gallery or Camera, add a photo, then Save. The moment appears on the timeline; the grid button in the top right switches to the grid view.
```

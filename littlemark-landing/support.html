Frequently asked questions

How do I save a moment?

Tap the + button, then take a photo or video, pick from your library, or record a voice note. Add a few words if you like, check the place, weather and suggested categories, and tap Save. A moment needs at least one photo, video or voice note.

Where are my moments stored?

Only on your iPhone, inside Littlemark. Nothing is uploaded and there is no account. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I back up my moments or move them to a new iPhone?

Littlemark's data is part of your iPhone backup. Turn on iCloud Backup (Settings → your name → iCloud → iCloud Backup) or back up to a computer, then restore that backup on your new iPhone. Deleting the app deletes its moments, so back up first.

Why does my voice note have no transcript?

Transcripts are made on your device so the audio never leaves it. Check that Speech Recognition is allowed in Settings → Littlemark. If your language has no on-device speech model, the voice note is saved without a transcript.

Why is there no place or weather on a moment?

Littlemark needs location access while you create the moment (Settings → Littlemark → Location → While Using the App) and an internet connection to look up the place and weather. You can also set the place by hand on the review screen.

How do I delete a moment?

Touch and hold the moment on the timeline or grid, then choose Delete. Its photos, videos and voice note are deleted too.

How do I change the language or theme?

Tap the gear icon on the main screen. Littlemark is available in English, Vietnamese and Japanese, with light, dark and system themes.

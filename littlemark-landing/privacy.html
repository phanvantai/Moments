Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

In short: Littlemark keeps your moments on your iPhone. There is no account, no server of ours, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. We do not collect any of your data.

1. Your moments stay on your iPhone

Everything you save in Littlemark — photos, videos, voice notes, text, tags, places and weather — is stored only in the app's storage on your device. Littlemark has no account system and no servers, and this version does not sync to iCloud. If you turn on iCloud Backup or back up your iPhone to a computer, that backup includes the app's data and is handled by Apple under your own settings.

2. Location and weather

With your permission, Littlemark reads your location only while you create or edit a moment. To show a place name and the weather, the coordinates are sent to Apple's services (Apple Maps for the place name and Apple Weather through WeatherKit). Apple processes them under the Apple Privacy Policy; we never receive them. You can turn location off for any moment, or deny the permission entirely.

3. Voice notes and transcription

Voice notes are recorded with the microphone and saved on your device. Transcripts are made with Apple's on-device speech recognition, so the audio never leaves your iPhone. If your language has no on-device model, the voice note is saved without a transcript.

4. Camera and photo library

The camera and photo library are used only to capture or pick the photos and videos you choose. A copy of each one is saved inside Littlemark. The app does not read the rest of your library.

5. Category suggestions

Suggested categories are worked out on your device with Apple's Vision and Natural Language frameworks. Nothing is uploaded for this.

6. No tracking, analytics or ads

Littlemark contains no third-party SDKs, analytics, advertising or tracking. It does not use the advertising identifier.

7. Deleting your data

Deleting a moment in the app also deletes its photos, videos and voice note. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device.

8. Children

Littlemark is a general-audience app. Because we do not collect any data, we do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. If a future version adds a feature such as iCloud sync, this page will explain it before it ships.

10. Contact
